# Frontier combat boards

`/frontiertop` and the guild menu open all-time player-kill and guild-vault boards. Player rows use heads and guild rows use their stored banners. Players with dialog support receive a dialog; an inventory is the fallback. Only scores recorded after installation are included.

Player deaths with a player killer increment that player's score; self-kills do not. Guild vault defeats are credited only when the attacker is identifiable, is not a defender, and belongs to exactly one guild. Unattributed TNT is deliberately not credited. These totals are not retroactive and are not an anti-farming system. Rankings exclude disbanded guilds from display.

Frontier scores persist separately in `frontier-combat-leaderboards.yml`. The claim outline command toggles the current viewer's particles, which otherwise expire after 60 seconds; claim previews are independent. Outside raids, Nether portal travel into another guild's claim and automatic portal creation touching it are blocked; guild members can still use their own portals. During raids, the portal restriction is lifted.

Initial verification: leaderboard persistence, self-credit, dialog capability, and portal-policy tests passed. In an isolated 26.4 lab, simulated player-death and vault-credit callbacks updated the dialog, rendered a head and guild banner, and survived restarts. A guild owner was allowed through an existing portal inside their claim; the same portal rejected the player after lab membership was removed, then allowed the outsider while the lab raid window was active. A synthetic portal-creation event touching the claim was cancelled for the outsider and allowed for the member. A real portal created wholly outside the claim was allowed. The inventory fallback was observed before the capability fix. Actual in-claim portal generation remains unverified in game. Changes have not been deployed to Bloom.

2026-11-12 follow-up: Dawn 26.3 displayed both translated dialogs, their player head and guild banners, switched categories, and returned to the Frontier guild menu after a navigation fix. In the isolated lab a Raiders guild member's simulated block-break events reached the real vault listener during an active raid; the test-only required-hit threshold was two. LabGuild's founding vault was cleared, its single claim was removed, vault status became unavailable, and Raiders received exactly one persisted vault score. A repeated event did not increment the score. The score and siege completion record remained after restart. TNT attribution, multi-chunk release, and an actual player-driven break remain untested in this follow-up. Bloom was unchanged.

2026-11-13 TNT follow-up: using real lab TNT entities spawned next to two separate founding vaults, each protecting two adjacent chunk partitions, with 20 required hits and four damage per explosion: five player-sourced blasts removed the Raiders vault and both partitions and credited LabGuild once; five source-less blasts removed the PowderTest vault and both partitions without changing any guild's score. Vaults were unavailable and both releases and scores remained after a server restart. These TNT entities were spawned by a lab probe; player ignition, redstone cannons and chained TNT attribution were not tested. Bloom was unchanged.
