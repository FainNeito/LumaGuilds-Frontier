Frontier living-world additions - SPEAR work record

Specification (2026-11-11)
FW-01: WHEN a player sends ordinary chat, RoseChat SHALL deliver it only within 200 blocks in the same world. Guild/private/staff messaging and intentional server announcements SHALL retain their existing audience.
FW-02: WHEN players join, leave, or die, Frontier SHALL suppress the vanilla global broadcast and send the event only to nearby players. Hidden players SHALL NOT be revealed to observers who cannot see them.
FW-03: WHEN no current member performs qualifying movement or interaction for 21 days, territory SHALL lose protection through the durable FrontierClaimRelease path. Existing territories SHALL receive a fresh grace period at first observation. Decay SHALL NOT delete blocks, inventories, vault contents, or start a raid replacement cooldown. Persistent state errors SHALL prevent expiry. Guild activity SHALL consider current membership, not a departed member's activity.
FW-04: WHEN remaining inactivity time crosses 7, 3, or 1 days, owners SHALL receive an in-game warning when online and opted-in Discord delivery when available. Returning players SHALL see pending warnings before their next qualifying action resets inactivity. Claims SHALL never expire while an owner/current member is online.
FW-05: WHEN an opted-in linked owner is raided or loses a vault, Frontier SHALL send a private Discord alert through the configured DiscordSRV account-link provider. Repeated attack messages SHALL be limited to one per recipient per 5 minutes; defeat is independent. Ordinary visitor messages SHALL NOT go to Discord. Missing provider, unlinking, or blocked DMs SHALL fail without affecting raids.
FW-06: WHEN above-sea-level Riptide succeeds, Frontier SHALL enforce a configurable 10-second player cooldown across tridents. Denied/cancelled use SHALL NOT restart the timer. Existing MaceGuard and CombatLogX restrictions/retags SHALL remain authoritative.
FW-07: WHEN an armored horse takes combat damage, configurable 20 percent additional protection SHALL apply without protecting its rider. Spear and TNT-minecart damage to players SHALL have separate configurable base-damage caps (16 and 20 health points); armor mitigation still applies. Vault TNT damage SHALL remain unchanged.
FW-08: /serverinfo, /rules and /claim help SHALL explain actual Frontier settings and commands. Existing one guild/solo home rules and combat restrictions SHALL remain unchanged.
